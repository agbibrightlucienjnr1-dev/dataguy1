Documentation / Transfer / Initiate Transfer
Initiate Transfer
POST
https://api.moolre.com/open/transact/transfer

This endpoint allows you to send money from your account to a recipient's mobile money or bank account instantly.

dangerous
Do not Assume Transfer Failure
Never assume a transfer has failed unless the transfer response or status check response txstatus indicates failure with a value of 2.

lightbulb
Validate Account Name before Transfer
We recommend that you validate account name before initiating a transfer to ensure you are sending to the right recipient.
